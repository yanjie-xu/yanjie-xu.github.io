News · Nanjing Forestry University · 2025-11-04

Exploring how birds respond to a changing world

During a visit to Nanjing Forestry University, Yanjie Xu presented research on how birds respond to climate change through shifts in morphology, phenology and distributions, as well as the implications for conservation and wildlife disease. The seminar brought together more than 40 students and researchers for discussion on climate change and adaptive evolution.

Press release · University of Helsinki · 2023-09-11

A changing climate reshapes wildlife pathogen risks

A European synthesis links the occurrence of many pathogens in birds and bats to temperature and rainfall. The associations vary among pathogens, suggesting that climate change could reshape infectious disease risks.

Mountains and coasts shape birds’ climate journeys

European bird communities are moving towards cooler places, but mountains and coastlines can obstruct their paths. These shifts are not keeping pace with warming, leaving some communities increasingly exposed.

Wintering farmland birds shift north fastest

Finnish bird surveys reveal that northward shifts differ between winter habitats. Farmland birds moved fastest, with changes in snow cover likely affecting their access to food.
