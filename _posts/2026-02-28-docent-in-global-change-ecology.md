I am pleased to share that I have been awarded the **Title of Docent in Global Change Ecology** by the Department of Biology at the University of Turku.

The title recognises research and teaching expertise across macroecology, biodiversity change, conservation prioritisation, and One Health. It also provides a new platform for collaboration, supervision, and teaching in global change ecology.
