About

I model ecological change at broad spatial and temporal scales.

I am a macroecologist working with large biodiversity datasets to understand species redistribution, community change, conservation priorities and wildlife–health connections.

Senior Research Scientist, Finnish Environment Institute (Syke)
Docent in Global Change Ecology, University of Turku

Research

Three connected directions.

01

Biodiversity dynamics

How climate and land-use change reshape distributions, phenology, communities and population stability.

02

Conservation

How spatial prioritisation and ecological networks can identify where conservation matters most.

03

One Health

How wildlife, environmental change and pathogen risk are connected across large spatial scales.
