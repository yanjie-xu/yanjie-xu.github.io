About

Modelling nature across space and time.

I am a macroecologist, fascinated by modelling the nature. I have been investigating macro-spatial ecological dynamics by modelling spatiotemporal biodiversity patterns with big data. I am particularly interested in conservation prioritization, human-wildlife interaction, and one-health topics.

Three research pillars

Patterns, priorities, and health

01

Biodiversity dynamics

Dynamics in distribution and stability of biodiversity

I investigate how land-use and climate change reshape species distributions, migration connectivity, phenology, community composition, and population stability across regional to continental scales.

02

Conservation priorities

Spatial prioritization for biodiversity conservation

I develop network and spatial-prioritisation approaches to identify important habitats, evaluate protected-area systems, and translate ecological evidence into conservation decisions.

03

One Health

Wildlife and zoonotic diseases

I model broad-scale pathogen and disease-risk patterns in birds, bats, and mammals, linking host diversity, life history, climate, habitat change, and movement within a One Health framework.
